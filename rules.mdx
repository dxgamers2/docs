Rules are the foundation of a good roleplay server. Without them, the immersive experience that makes FLSRP worth playing breaks down quickly. These rules exist to protect the quality of every player's experience — not just your own. Read them in full before submitting a whitelist application, and refer back to them whenever you're unsure whether an action is acceptable.

<Warning>
  Rule violations can result in a verbal warning, a temporary kick, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban depending on the severity and frequency of the offence. Staff apply penalties at their discretion based on the circumstances.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Staff decisions are final. If you believe a ban was issued in error, you may submit a formal ban appeal through the [ban appeals process](/support/ban-appeals). Appeals are reviewed by senior staff and must be made in good faith.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="General conduct">
    FLSRP is a community where players from different backgrounds share the same space. Everyone is expected to behave respectfully and with maturity at all times — both in-game and in Discord.

    - **Respect all players and staff.** Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, and targeted personal attacks are not tolerated in any form, on any platform.
    - **Act with maturity.** You are expected to handle disputes calmly. If you have a problem with another player's behaviour, report it to staff rather than escalating in-game.
    - **No trolling or griefing.** Actions taken purely to annoy, disrupt, or ruin another player's experience — with no legitimate roleplay justification — are prohibited.
    - **Follow staff instructions.** When a staff member issues an instruction during or after a situation, comply promptly. Arguing with staff in-game is not permitted; use the appropriate Discord channels to raise concerns afterward.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Roleplay standards">
    FLSRP expects a consistent standard of immersive, character-driven roleplay. Your character is a person living in the city — act accordingly.

    - **Stay in character.** Once you are in the server, you are your character. Do not break immersion by speaking as yourself or referencing the game mechanics around you, except in designated out-of-character channels (see Communication rules).
    - **No metagaming.** Metagaming means using information your character could not realistically know — such as information from Discord, a stream, or another player's perspective — to influence in-game decisions. Your character only knows what they have witnessed or been told within the roleplay.
    - **No powergaming.** Powergaming means forcing outcomes on other players without giving them a fair chance to respond, or performing actions that are physically impossible for your character. Always give other players the opportunity to react and participate in a scene.
    - **Roleplay all situations to their conclusion.** Do not log off, disconnect, or leave an active scene to avoid consequences. Combat logging — disconnecting during or immediately after a hostile encounter — is a serious violation.
    - **Value your character's life.** Your character wants to survive. Do not behave recklessly or accept death casually — especially in interactions with armed players or law enforcement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Combat rules">
    All combat on FLSRP must have a clear, legitimate roleplay justification. Violence for its own sake has no place on the server.

    - **No RDM (random deathmatch).** RDM means attacking or killing another player without any prior roleplay interaction or valid in-character reason. You cannot shoot someone simply because they are near you, have something you want, or you feel like it. Every hostile action must be preceded by meaningful roleplay.
    - **No VDM (vehicle deathmatch).** VDM means using a vehicle as a weapon to intentionally injure or kill another player without roleplay justification. Accidentally clipping someone in traffic is not VDM — deliberately running players over is.
    - **Initiation is required.** Before engaging in hostile roleplay that could lead to injury or death, you must give the other player a reasonable opportunity to comply or respond. Ambushes are permitted in certain criminal contexts, but must follow established gang or heist roleplay — they are not a loophole for RDM.
    - **Hostage situations.** If you take a player hostage, you are responsible for their wellbeing as a roleplay participant. Do not leave them in a situation where they cannot continue to play.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="New Life Rule (NLR)">
    The New Life Rule governs what your character remembers after they die. It exists to make death meaningful and prevent revenge cycles that break immersion.

    - **After your character dies**, you begin a new life. Your character does not remember the events that led to their death — including who killed them, where the incident took place, or what was said or done in the moments before.
    - **You may not return to the location of your death** for at least 15 minutes after respawning, and you may not return to interfere in the same ongoing situation.
    - **You may not seek revenge** for your death or act on any knowledge your previous life had about the people or events involved in the incident.
    - **NLR does not apply to staff-supervised admin sits** or situations where staff have explicitly suspended it for a specific scene.

    NLR is not a punishment — it is a storytelling mechanic. Use it as an opportunity to explore how your character moves forward, not as a rule to resent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Communication">
    All in-game communication is expected to be in character unless you use the designated out-of-character channel. Breaking immersion through out-of-character speech disrupts everyone in earshot.

    - **Use `/ooc` for out-of-character communication** in-game. This is reserved for genuine technical issues, urgent clarifications, or confirming consent in sensitive roleplay situations. Do not use `/ooc` for casual conversation or to comment on roleplay you dislike.
    - **No mic spam.** Do not use soundboards, music playback, excessive background noise, or any audio that disrupts voice communication for other players.
    - **Speak clearly and in character.** Use a realistic voice for your character. Constantly breaking into laughter, shouting, or speaking in an unintelligible way degrades the experience for those around you.
    - **Keep Discord text channels on-topic.** Use the correct channels for applications, support tickets, and general chat. Do not post in channels you are not meant to use.
    - **No doxxing or sharing personal information.** Sharing real-life personal information about any player — with or without their consent — is an immediate bannable offence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cheating & exploits">
    FLSRP operates a zero-tolerance policy toward cheating, hacking, and the exploitation of bugs or unintended game mechanics.

    - **No cheats or hacks.** Using any third-party software, trainer, mod menu, or tool that gives you an unfair advantage — including aim assistance, speed hacks, money hacks, or invincibility — results in an immediate and permanent ban with no possibility of appeal.
    - **No exploiting bugs.** If you discover a bug or unintended mechanic that provides an advantage — such as item duplication, money generation, or bypassing locked areas — you must report it to staff immediately. Exploiting known bugs is treated the same as cheating.
    - **No account sharing or ban evasion.** Sharing your account with another player, or creating a new account to circumvent a ban, results in a permanent ban for all accounts involved.
    - **Report cheaters.** If you suspect another player is cheating, submit a report through the designated Discord channel with evidence. Do not attempt to expose or confront cheaters in-game.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
