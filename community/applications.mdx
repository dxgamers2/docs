FLSRP uses a two-stage application process to maintain a high-quality roleplay environment. First, you must complete the whitelist application to gain access to the server. Once you are whitelisted and have spent time in the community, you can apply separately for whitelisted job roles such as Police, EMS, Mechanic, or Lawyer. These are distinct processes — you cannot apply for a job role until your whitelist application has been approved.

<Warning>
  Copy-pasted, template-filled, or clearly low-effort applications are automatically denied. Staff review every submission and can identify generic answers. Take the time to write original, thoughtful responses.
</Warning>

## Whitelist application

The whitelist application is your first step to accessing the server. It takes roughly 10–20 minutes to complete thoroughly. Rush it and you will likely be denied.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord server">
    If you have not already, join the official FLSRP Discord server and complete the verification process. You need the **Member** role before you can access the application channel. See the [Discord](/community/discord) page for step-by-step instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rules channel thoroughly">
    Navigate to **#rules** and read every rule carefully. The application form includes a rules quiz, and your answers must demonstrate genuine understanding — not a summary you skimmed. Staff will deny applications that show a lack of rule knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to #whitelist-applications">
    Open the **#whitelist-applications** channel in Discord. Read the pinned messages and any instructions posted there before clicking the application form link. The pinned messages may contain recent updates or additional requirements not covered in this guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the application form">
    Complete all sections of the application form honestly and in full:

    - **Personal info** — your in-game name, age, and Discord username
    - **Roleplay experience** — describe your RP background, servers you have played on, and your preferred style of roleplay
    - **Character concept** — outline your character's name, backstory, personality, and goals in the city; the more developed, the better
    - **Rule quiz** — answer the scenario-based rule questions in your own words, demonstrating that you understand both the letter and spirit of each rule
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and wait for a decision">
    Submit the form and allow **24–48 hours** for staff to review your application. Do not open a support ticket to ask about your application status during this window — doing so may negatively affect your application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If approved — receive your whitelist role">
    If your application is approved, a staff member will assign you the **Whitelisted** role in Discord and post the server connection details. Follow the posted instructions to connect to the FiveM server for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If denied — review feedback and reapply">
    If your application is denied, staff will provide feedback explaining why. Read this feedback carefully. You may reapply after **7 days** from the date of denial. Use the waiting period to improve the weak areas identified in the feedback before submitting a new application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The strongest applications share three qualities: **authenticity** (write in your own voice, not what you think staff want to hear), **rule knowledge** (answer quiz questions with specific examples, not vague summaries), and **a developed character concept** (give your character a real backstory, clear motivations, and a reason to be in the city). A character concept that reads like a fully realized person rather than a blank template stands out immediately.
</Tip>
