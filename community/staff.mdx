The FLSRP staff team is responsible for keeping the server fair, safe, and enjoyable for everyone. The team operates across multiple tiers, each with a defined scope of responsibility — from high-level server direction down to day-to-day player support in Discord. Understanding who does what helps you reach the right person through the right channel, and get your issue resolved faster.

<Note>
  All staff members are volunteers who contribute their time outside of their own personal schedules. Response times may vary depending on availability. If your ticket is not answered immediately, please be patient — the team will get to it.
</Note>

## Staff roles and responsibilities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Owner">
    The Owner sets the overall direction of the server, makes final decisions on major policy changes, and has ultimate authority over all staff and community matters. The Owner is not typically the first point of contact for player issues — escalate to this level only in cases of serious staff misconduct that has not been addressed at lower levels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Head Admin">
    Head Admins oversee the entire admin team, ensure policies are applied consistently, and handle escalated issues that Admins cannot resolve. They are responsible for enforcing staff conduct standards and reviewing contested decisions. If you believe an Admin has acted inappropriately, the Head Admin is the correct escalation point.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Admin">
    Admins handle the most significant player-facing enforcement actions, including permanent bans, whitelist reviews, and complex conflict resolution between players. They also review escalated moderation decisions and assist with appeals. If a Moderator's action is disputed, an Admin will review the case.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Moderator">
    Moderators enforce the server rules in-game in real time. They issue warnings, temporary bans, and other corrective actions for rule violations during active roleplay sessions. If you witness a rule violation happening in-game, a Moderator is the appropriate person to involve through a support ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Support Staff">
    Support Staff are the first point of contact for most community members. They handle Discord support tickets, answer questions about the application process, assist new players with getting started, and triage issues before escalating them to the appropriate higher staff tier. When in doubt, open a ticket — Support Staff will direct you to the right place.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to contact staff

All official communication with staff happens through Discord support tickets. This ensures your request is tracked, timestamped, and visible to the appropriate team members — none of which is possible in a direct message.

1. Navigate to the **#support-tickets** channel in the FLSRP Discord server.
2. Click the button or follow the instructions to open a new ticket.
3. Select the category that best matches your issue (e.g., bug report, player report, ban appeal, general question).
4. Describe your issue clearly and completely in the ticket. Include any relevant evidence such as screenshots or clip links.
5. Wait for a staff member to respond. Do not create multiple tickets for the same issue.

<Warning>
  Do not abuse the support ticket system. Submitting false reports, filing duplicate tickets, or using tickets to harass staff are all violations of community rules and will result in disciplinary action. The ticket system exists to help players — misusing it delays legitimate requests from others.
</Warning>

## Reporting staff misconduct

If a staff member has acted inappropriately — such as abusing their powers, making biased decisions, or behaving in a way that violates community rules — you have a clear escalation path.

1. **Do not argue in public channels.** Bring the issue to the appropriate authority privately through a ticket.
2. **Open a support ticket** and clearly state that you are reporting a staff misconduct issue. Describe the incident with as much detail as possible, including the staff member's username, what happened, when it happened, and any evidence you have.
3. **If the misconduct involves an Admin or lower**, the ticket will be escalated to a Head Admin for review.
4. **If the misconduct involves a Head Admin**, address your ticket to the Owner. Clearly state in the ticket that you are escalating to Owner level.

All misconduct reports are treated seriously. Providing false or exaggerated reports against staff members is itself a rule violation and will be handled accordingly.
