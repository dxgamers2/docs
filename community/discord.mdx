The FLSRP Discord server is the central hub for everything related to the community — from submitting your whitelist application and getting server updates to opening support tickets and chatting with other players. Before you can connect to the server in-game, you must join Discord, get verified, and complete the whitelist application process.

<Note>
  Discord verification is required before you can connect to the FLSRP FiveM server. You will not receive connection details until your whitelist application has been approved.
</Note>

## Key channels

Familiarize yourself with these channels as soon as you join. Each one serves a specific purpose and has its own rules.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="#announcements">
    This is the official channel for server updates, patch notes, event announcements, and policy changes. All major news comes through here first. Enable notifications for this channel so you never miss a critical update.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="#rules">
    The community rules are pinned here. Read this channel thoroughly before submitting any application — staff will expect you to demonstrate knowledge of these rules. The rules channel is updated whenever policies change, so check back periodically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="#whitelist-applications">
    This is where you submit your whitelist application form to request access to the server. The channel contains the application form link and submission instructions. Only post here when you are ready to apply; do not use it for general questions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="#job-applications">
    Once you are whitelisted, you can apply for faction and whitelisted job roles — such as Police, EMS, Mechanic, and Lawyer — through this channel. Job applications are a separate process from the initial whitelist application and are only open to already-whitelisted players.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="#support-tickets">
    Open a support ticket here if you need help with account issues, have a bug to report, want to appeal a ban, or need to report a rule violation. Do not DM staff members directly — all official communication goes through tickets so it is tracked and handled properly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="#general">
    The main out-of-character community chat. Conversations here must be in English. Do not use this channel for in-character roleplay — keep all RP in-game. Treat other members with respect; the zero-tolerance policy for harassment and toxic behavior applies here.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to join and get verified

Follow these steps to go from a new Discord member to a verified community member who can begin the whitelist application.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord server">
    Use the official FLSRP invite link shared through G2 Esports channels or the FLSRP website to join the server. Make sure you are joining the correct server — look for the G2 Esports branding in the server icon and name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the rules">
    When you first join, you will land in the welcome channel. Read the displayed community rules and click the confirm or agree button (or react with the designated emoji) to accept them. This unlocks access to the rest of the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Discord verification">
    Some verification bots may ask you to solve a CAPTCHA or complete a short verification step to confirm you are a real person. Follow the prompts in the verification channel until you receive confirmation that verification is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the Member role">
    Once you have accepted the rules and passed verification, the bot will automatically assign you the **Member** role. This role grants you access to the general channels, including #whitelist-applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin your whitelist application">
    Navigate to **#whitelist-applications** and follow the instructions there to submit your application. See the [Applications](/community/applications) page for a full walkthrough of the application process.
  </Step>
</Steps>
