Before opening a support ticket, check this page first — most common questions are answered here. If you still can't find what you're looking for after reading through the FAQs below, head to our Discord and open a ticket in the #support-tickets channel.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I join the server?">
    FLSRP is a whitelisted server, which means you must apply and be approved before you can connect. Follow these steps to get started:

    1. Join the [FLSRP Discord server](https://discord.gg/flsrp) and read the rules in #rules.
    2. Navigate to the whitelist application channel and submit your application.
    3. Once approved, you'll receive a confirmation in Discord and can connect to the server using the FiveM client.

    You can find the server by searching "FLSRP" in the FiveM server browser, or use the direct connect option with the server IP pinned in Discord.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is FLSRP free to play?">
    Playing on FLSRP is free, but you do need a legitimate copy of Grand Theft Auto V. FiveM itself is also free to download and install. GTA V is available on Steam, the Epic Games Store, and the Rockstar Games Launcher — any of these versions is compatible.

    Some cosmetic or supporter perks may be available through donations, but all core gameplay content is accessible to every whitelisted player at no cost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need to install?">
    To play on FLSRP you need two things:

    - **Grand Theft Auto V** — purchased and installed through Steam, Epic Games Store, or the Rockstar Games Launcher.
    - **FiveM client** — the free multiplayer mod framework. Download it from [fivem.net](https://fivem.net) and run the installer. FiveM will automatically detect your GTA V installation.

    Once both are installed, launch FiveM (not GTA V directly) to access the server browser and connect to FLSRP.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does whitelist approval take?">
    Whitelist applications are reviewed by staff manually. Review times vary depending on application volume, but you can generally expect a response within **24 to 72 hours**. During busy periods — such as after major updates or community events — it may take a bit longer.

    <Note>
      Do not submit duplicate applications. Submitting multiple applications for the same account may result in your application being moved to the bottom of the queue or rejected.
    </Note>

    You'll receive a notification in Discord once your application has been reviewed. Make sure your Discord DMs are open or that you're monitoring the application status channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple characters?">
    Yes. FLSRP supports multiple character slots per player account. Each character is treated as a completely separate individual with their own name, backstory, inventory, money, and job history. You cannot transfer items, money, or progress between characters — each one starts fresh.

    To switch characters, use the character selection screen when you first connect or via the in-game phone/menu, depending on the current system. Check the #game-guides channel in Discord for the latest instructions on switching characters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I make money starting out?">
    As a new character, there are several legal entry-level jobs available that don't require any application or whitelist:

    - **Fishing** — head to the docks and pick up a fishing rod from the nearby shop.
    - **Trucking / delivery jobs** — pick up delivery contracts from the logistics center.
    - **Mining and foraging** — gather raw materials and sell them to processing locations.
    - **Hunting** — purchase a hunting license and head to the designated hunting zones.

    Focus on legal jobs first to build your bank balance and reputation before exploring other opportunities. Check #economy-guides in Discord for the most up-to-date money-making tips.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the New Life Rule (NLR)?">
    The New Life Rule (NLR) states that when your character dies, they forget everything that happened in the roleplay scenario that led to their death. Specifically:

    - You may not return to the location where you died for **15 minutes** after respawning.
    - You may not seek revenge on the players involved in your death.
    - You may not use any information your character knew before dying to influence your roleplay after respawning — your character effectively has no memory of the events leading to their death.

    Violating NLR is a serious rule breach and can result in a warning, kick, or ban. If you're unsure whether something counts as an NLR violation, err on the side of caution and ask a staff member.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I apply for a whitelisted job like police or EMS?">
    Whitelisted jobs — including the Los Santos Police Department (LSPD), Emergency Medical Services (EMS), and other factions — have their own separate application processes. To apply:

    1. Navigate to the relevant application channel in Discord (e.g., #lspd-applications or #ems-applications).
    2. Read the pinned requirements carefully. Most departments require a minimum number of hours played on the server.
    3. Fill out and submit the application form.
    4. Wait for the department's hiring staff to review and respond.

    Each faction manages its own roster and hiring pace, so response times vary. Do not ping department staff to ask about your application status — this can negatively affect your chances.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report another player for breaking rules?">
    If you witness a rule violation, do not escalate the situation in-game. Instead, report it through the proper channels:

    - **In-game**: Use the `/report` command followed by the player's name and a brief description of the issue.
    - **Discord**: Open a player report ticket in the #support-tickets channel and include the offending player's name, a description of what happened, and any evidence (screenshots or video clips).

    Reports are reviewed by staff as soon as possible. Providing video evidence significantly speeds up the process and increases the likelihood of action being taken.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I stream or record gameplay on FLSRP?">
    Yes, you are welcome to stream and record your FLSRP gameplay. However, you must follow these guidelines:

    - Do not stream or record other players without their consent if doing so would expose their out-of-character (OOC) information.
    - Do not stream snipe — using your stream or a viewer's information to gain an in-game advantage is a bannable offense.
    - Respect the server's rules on the content you broadcast. Streamers are held to the same conduct standards as all other players.

    If you are a content creator and want a dedicated content creator role or any additional support, open a ticket in Discord.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I get disconnected mid-RP?">
    Disconnections happen. If you drop from the server during an active roleplay scenario, reconnect as quickly as possible and reach out to the other players involved — ideally over Discord — to let them know what happened and to resume the scene.

    If you were disconnected during a high-stakes situation (robbery, arrest, etc.), inform the staff or the other players so the scene can be handled fairly. Do not abuse reconnecting to escape consequences in-game.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I bring items or money from other servers?">
    No. FLSRP runs on its own independent economy and database. Nothing from other FiveM servers carries over, and attempting to duplicate, exploit, or inject items or currency is considered cheating and will result in a permanent ban.

    All in-game wealth and items must be earned through legitimate gameplay within FLSRP.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
