If you've been banned from FLSRP and believe the ban was issued in error, or you want to acknowledge your mistake and request a second chance, you can submit a formal appeal through the official Discord ticket system. All appeals are reviewed by senior staff, and every player is entitled to one appeal per ban unless otherwise noted.

<Warning>
  Appeals for **permanent bans issued due to cheating, hacking, or exploiting** are not accepted. If your ban reason explicitly states cheating or use of unauthorized modifications, your appeal will be closed without review.
</Warning>

## Appeal process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the FLSRP Discord server">
    If you haven't already been banned from the Discord itself, join the server using the invite link on the FLSRP website or a community post. If you were banned from Discord as well as the game server, contact a community manager through a secondary method (such as reaching out to a publicly listed staff member on social media) to request a Discord re-entry for the purpose of submitting your appeal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to #support-tickets">
    Once you're in the Discord server, go to the **#support-tickets** channel. This channel is where all player-facing support interactions — including ban appeals — are handled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a new ticket and select 'Ban Appeal'">
    Click the button to create a new ticket. When prompted to choose a category, select **Ban Appeal**. A private ticket channel will be created for you and will only be visible to you and the staff team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your appeal with all required information">
    Your appeal must include the following details for staff to locate your record and make a fair decision:

    - **In-game character name** — the name of the character that was active when you were banned.
    - **Date of the ban** — the approximate date and time you were banned.
    - **Stated ban reason** — the reason shown in your ban message or communicated by staff.
    - **Your appeal statement** — explain what happened from your perspective, acknowledge any role you played in the incident, and make clear that you understand the rules and are committed to following them going forward.

    <Tip>
      Write your appeal as if you're speaking to someone who doesn't know you. Be specific, be honest, and be respectful. Vague appeals like "I didn't do anything wrong" are rarely overturned. Acknowledge what happened — even if you disagree with how staff handled it — explain your perspective calmly, and commit to better behavior. Appeals that show genuine understanding of the rules and accountability carry the most weight.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait up to 72 hours for a staff review">
    After submitting your appeal, a member of the senior staff team will review your record, the evidence on file, and your statement. Reviews are completed within **72 hours** in most cases, though this may be longer during peak periods or public holidays. Do not ping staff members to ask about the status of your appeal — doing so may result in a longer wait or the appeal being dismissed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the outcome in your ticket">
    Once a decision has been made, staff will respond directly in your ticket channel with the outcome and any next steps. Keep your Discord notifications on so you don't miss the response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Possible outcomes

<Note>
  There are three possible outcomes for a ban appeal:

  - **Ban upheld** — the ban stands as originally issued. No further action is taken at this time.
  - **Ban duration reduced** — the original ban length is shortened at staff's discretion. You'll be given a specific date when you can return.
  - **Ban overturned** — the ban is removed and your account is restored to good standing. You may rejoin the server immediately.
</Note>

## If your appeal is denied

<Info>
  If your appeal is denied, you must wait **30 days** before submitting another appeal for the same ban. Submitting a new ticket before the cooldown period has passed will result in the ticket being closed without review. Use the waiting period to reflect on the feedback provided by staff and prepare a stronger, more thoughtful appeal.
</Info>
