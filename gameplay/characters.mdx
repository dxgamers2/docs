Your character is the foundation of everything you do in FLSRP. Unlike your real identity or your FiveM account, your character is a fully separate persona — complete with their own name, appearance, history, and personality. Taking time to build a well-rounded character pays off in richer interactions with other players and a more immersive roleplay experience for everyone on the server.

## First-time character creation

When you connect to FLSRP for the first time, you are walked through the character creation process before you can enter the world. Follow the steps below to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your character's name">
    Choose a realistic first and last name that fits the GTA V setting. Avoid names that reference real-world celebrities, other game characters, or anything that breaks immersion. Your character's name is permanent — choose carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design your appearance">
    Use the GTA V character creator to build your character's look. Adjust facial features, hair, and skin tone to match the identity you have in mind. Your appearance is visible to every other player in the world, so take the time to make it feel authentic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your character backstory">
    You are prompted to provide a brief backstory before entering the server. This does not need to be a novel — a few sentences covering where your character grew up, what brought them to Los Santos, and what they are trying to do with their life is enough to ground your roleplay. See the tips below for guidance on writing a strong backstory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a starting appearance and clothing">
    After your character is created, head to a clothing store to set your starting outfit. Your clothing is part of your character's identity and visible to other players at all times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the world">
    Once setup is complete, you spawn into Los Santos as your character. Everything from this point forward — your inventory, your money, your properties — is tied to this character and saved between sessions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multiple characters

FLSRP allows you to create more than one character on a single account. Each character has a completely separate identity, inventory, bank balance, and property ownership. Switching between characters requires you to disconnect and reconnect, selecting the character you want to play at the login screen. Characters do not share money, items, or knowledge — what one character knows, the other does not.

## Character persistence

Everything your character earns, owns, and carries is saved automatically between sessions. When you log off and reconnect, your character returns exactly where they left off with the same inventory, cash, and owned properties. You do not need to do anything special to save your progress.

## Writing a strong backstory

Your backstory shapes how other players perceive and interact with your character. A realistic, grounded backstory makes it easier for you to stay in character and for others to engage with you meaningfully.

<Tip>
  A good backstory answers three questions: Where did your character come from? What brought them to Los Santos? What do they want? Keep it grounded in the GTA V world — avoid supernatural abilities, military super-soldier backgrounds, or any history that would give your character an unrealistic advantage. The most compelling characters have ordinary lives with believable motivations.
</Tip>

Some guidelines to follow when writing your backstory:

- **Keep it realistic.** Your character should fit naturally into the GTA V world. Think working-class upbringings, financial pressures, family conflict, or ambition — not secret government training or extraordinary wealth with no explanation.
- **Leave room to grow.** A backstory that explains everything leaves no room for discovery. Build in some open questions that can be answered through actual roleplay.
- **Avoid power fantasies.** Characters with elite combat training, connections to every criminal organisation, or inexplicable resources are difficult to roleplay around and often frustrate other players.
- **Make it specific.** "Grew up poor" is vague. "Grew up in Chamberlain Hills, dropped out of high school to support a sick parent, and moved to the city looking for a fresh start" is specific and immediately useful for RP interactions.

## The New Life Rule (NLR)

FLSRP enforces the New Life Rule. If your character is killed, they lose all memory of the events that directly led to their death — the conflict, the people involved, and the location. Your character is not erased; they wake up at the hospital with no recollection of what happened.

<Warning>
  The New Life Rule is strictly enforced. Returning to the scene of your death, seeking revenge on the players who killed you, or acting on any knowledge your character would not have after being revived is a rule violation. If you are unsure whether something falls under NLR, check the server rules or ask a staff member.
</Warning>

The NLR exists to keep roleplay scenarios from escalating indefinitely and to give all parties a clean reset after an encounter ends. Respecting it is part of being a good member of the FLSRP community.
