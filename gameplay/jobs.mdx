Jobs are how your character earns a living in FLSRP. Whether you want a quiet life hauling cargo between warehouses or you are aiming for a badge and a patrol car, the server offers a range of careers to suit different playstyles. Some jobs are open to anyone who shows up and clocks in; others require a formal application, an interview, and structured training before you can take on the role.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Civilian jobs">
    Civilian jobs are available to every player from the moment they join the server. You do not need to apply or be approved — simply find the job location on the map, interact with the NPC or terminal, and start working. These roles are designed to help new players earn their first dollars, learn the map, and get comfortable with the server before committing to a whitelisted career.

    | Job | Description | Approximate pay |
    |---|---|---|
    | Taxi driver | Pick up and drop off passengers around Los Santos using the taxi dispatch system. Pay depends on trip distance. | $80–$220 per fare |
    | Delivery driver | Transport packages and goods between businesses and drop-off points across the city and Blaine County. | $100–$300 per delivery |
    | Garbage collector | Drive a garbage truck along designated routes, stopping at bins to collect waste. A reliable, steady income for new players. | $150–$250 per route |
    | Fisher | Head to the coast or inland lakes with a rod and sell your catch at the local market. Fish prices vary by type and rarity. | $50–$400 per session |
    | Miner | Travel to the mine in Blaine County to extract ore and sell it at the processing facility. Physical and time-intensive, but the pay reflects that. | $200–$500 per haul |
    | Farmer | Tend crops and manage livestock on the outskirts of the map. Harvested goods are sold at markets for variable prices depending on supply. | $150–$450 per harvest |

    Civilian jobs pay out based on how much work you complete in a session. The more time and effort you put in, the more you earn. They are a great way to build up savings before pursuing a whitelisted position.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Whitelisted jobs">
    Whitelisted jobs are high-responsibility roles that have a direct and lasting impact on other players' experiences. They require you to submit a formal application through the FLSRP Discord and pass a review process before you can access the role in-game. Each faction maintains its own hiring standards and expects applicants to demonstrate an understanding of both the server rules and the responsibilities of the role.

    | Job | Description | Faction |
    |---|---|---|
    | LSPD officer | Patrol the city, respond to criminal activity, conduct arrests, and uphold the law. Officers must follow use-of-force guidelines and proper arrest procedures at all times. | Los Santos Police Department |
    | EMS / paramedic | Respond to medical callouts, revive injured players, and transport critically injured characters to the hospital. EMS personnel are protected from being intentionally targeted while on duty. | Emergency Medical Services |
    | Mechanic | Repair and modify player vehicles at licensed garages. Mechanics can also respond to roadside breakdown callouts dispatched through the mechanic radio. | Bennies / licensed garages |
    | Lawyer | Represent players in in-character legal proceedings, advise clients on their rights during police interactions, and argue cases before the court. | The Bar Association |

    <Note>
      Being accepted into a whitelisted faction is only the first step. Each faction runs its own onboarding process, which includes in-character training sessions, ride-alongs or shadowed shifts, and a probationary period before you receive full access to the role's tools and permissions. Expect to spend at least a week in training before operating independently.
    </Note>

    ## How to apply

    All whitelisted job applications are submitted and reviewed through the FLSRP Discord. Read the pinned instructions in the relevant faction channel before applying, and make sure your application is complete — incomplete applications are typically declined without review.
