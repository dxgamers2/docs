FLSRP is the official G2 Esports FiveM roleplay server, built for players who want a serious, story-driven GTA V multiplayer experience. Running on FiveM — a free GTA V multiplayer modification — FLSRP gives you access to a persistent living world with civilian careers, law enforcement, emergency services, criminal organisations, and a fully player-driven economy. Every dollar you earn, every job you take, and every interaction you have shapes the world around you.

## What makes FLSRP different

FLSRP is a **whitelisted** server. That means you apply before you join, and every player you meet has agreed to uphold the same standard of roleplay. There are no random griefers or trolls — just a community that takes its craft seriously. The G2 Esports brand backs an active staff team dedicated to enforcing the rules fairly and keeping the server healthy.

### Key features

- **Whitelisted community** — every player goes through an application and approval process, maintaining a consistent standard of roleplay quality
- **Diverse job system** — choose from open civilian jobs you can start immediately, or work toward whitelisted roles like police officer, EMS paramedic, or licensed mechanic
- **Player economy** — earn in-game dollars through legitimate work or other means; spend them on vehicles, properties, and services provided by other players
- **Active staff** — a dedicated moderation team handles reports, ban appeals, and rule enforcement around the clock
