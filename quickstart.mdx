Getting onto FLSRP takes a few steps, but the process is straightforward. You need to install FiveM, join the Discord server, and pass a whitelist review before you can connect. This guide walks you through everything in order so you don't miss a step.

<Note>
  FLSRP is a whitelisted server. You cannot connect until your application is reviewed and approved by the staff team. Approval typically takes **24–48 hours** after you submit.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Read the [server rules](/rules) before you write your application. Staff reviewers expect applicants to be familiar with the rules, and knowing them upfront will save you from common mistakes once you're in-game.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install FiveM">
    FiveM is a free multiplayer modification for GTA V that lets you connect to community-run servers like FLSRP. You need a legitimate copy of GTA V installed before FiveM will work.

    1. Go to [fivem.net](https://fivem.net) and download the FiveM client for your operating system.
    2. Run the installer and let it download the required files. This may take a few minutes depending on your connection.
    3. Launch FiveM once installation is complete to confirm it opens without errors.

    <Info>
      FiveM does not modify your GTA V installation. Your single-player game and existing saves are unaffected.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join the Discord server">
    Discord is the primary hub for FLSRP. You need to be a member to submit your whitelist application, receive announcements, and contact staff.

    1. Open Discord or download it from [discord.com](https://discord.com) if you don't have it.
    2. Join the G2 Esports Discord using the invite link pinned in the FLSRP announcements channel or available at [discord.gg/g2esports](https://discord.gg/g2esports).
    3. Read the `#rules` and `#announcements` channels to familiarise yourself with community expectations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit a whitelist application">
    Once you're in Discord, locate the whitelist application channel. Applications are reviewed by the staff team, so take time to write thoughtful responses.

    1. Navigate to the `#apply` channel (or the channel designated for whitelist applications).
    2. Follow the posted instructions to open an application form.
    3. Fill in all required fields — this typically includes your roleplay experience, a character backstory concept, and confirmation that you've read the rules.
    4. Submit your application and wait for a response in your application thread.

    <Warning>
      Incomplete or low-effort applications are rejected. Write clearly, be specific about your roleplay background, and demonstrate that you understand the rules.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    After submitting, a staff member will review your application. The typical turnaround is **24–48 hours**, though it may be shorter during active periods.

    - Watch your Discord notifications for a response in your application thread.
    - Staff may ask follow-up questions — answer them promptly to avoid delays.
    - If your application is declined, you will receive a reason and information on when you may reapply.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect to the server">
    Once your application is approved, you're whitelisted and can connect to FLSRP immediately.

    1. Open FiveM and click **Play**.
    2. In the server browser, search for **FLSRP** or paste the server IP into the direct connect field.
    3. Click **Connect** and wait for the server to load your resources. The first connection may take a few minutes while assets download.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your character">
    When you first spawn in, you are taken through a character creation screen before entering the world.

    1. Set your character's appearance using the in-game editor — this is how other players will see you.
    2. Enter your character's name. Use a realistic, roleplay-appropriate name; your own name or joke names are not permitted.
    3. Choose your character's starting background. This affects some initial dialogue options and is visible to staff.
    4. Confirm your character to enter the world and begin your roleplay.

    <Tip>
      Give your character a backstory before you start playing — even a few sentences about who they are and why they're in the city. Strong characters lead to better roleplay for everyone.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
